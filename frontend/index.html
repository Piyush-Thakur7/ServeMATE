About ServeMate

Our Mission & Parent Brand Core Principles

ServeMate is a community-powered social impact platform built under the parent brand Resence (resence.in).

We believe giving should not be a silent, one-off event. By combining Verified NGOs, Student Communities, and Gamified Levels, we build a trusted social layer where young people can contribute together, track real-world impact through proof-based accountability, and get recognized for their actions.

What makes ServeMate transparent? ▼
ServeMate tracks every single rupee. Unlike traditional sites, funds are routed directly to assigned cause NGOs. We then require registered volunteers to complete the work and upload verified YouTube video footage, which is cataloged on the public ledger.
Can anyone register as a volunteer? ▼
No. Volunteers belong to verified NGOs. Individuals cannot randomly sign up; they must request registration or be added by approved NGO partners, and administrators verify credentials.

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 17, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you provide to us directly when creating an account, donating, or applying as an NGO partner. This includes your name, email address, password hash, and metadata associated with proof submissions (such as GPS coordinates, timestamp, and device details for video verifications).

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to verify donations, compute community leaderboards, rank impact scores, generate personalized AI recommendations via the Gemini API, and display verified proof videos on the public Impact Ledger. We do not sell or lease your data to third parties.

3. Payment Processing Security

All donation payments are processed securely through our payment gateway partner, Razorpay. ServeMATE does not store your card details, UPI credentials, or banking passwords. All transactions comply with standard payment security guidelines.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions or data deletion requests, please contact our privacy officer at info@resence.in.

Terms of Service

Last Updated: June 17, 2026

1. Platform Agreement

By registering an account or making donations on ServeMATE (resence.in), you agree to comply with these terms, our privacy policy, and all applicable Indian laws regulating micro-donations and charitable organizations.

2. Campaign Funding & Release Mechanism

ServeMATE operates on a per-campaign target model. Micro-donations (starting at ₹10) are accumulated towards each campaign's specific target goal over a 7-10 day collection window per NGO. Upon campaign completion (or window expiration), 97-98% of collected funds (after a 2-3% platform & payment processing fee) are released directly to the designated NGO for campaign execution.

3. NGO & Volunteer Proof Requirements

All NGO partners agree to use only the in-app ServeMATE camera system to capture real-time, geotagged, and timestamped video proof of campaign execution. Uploading pre-recorded, edited, or un-geotagged media is strictly prohibited and constitutes grounds for immediate de-verification and platform ban.

4. User Conduct & Abuse

Users must not manipulate the leaderboard, exploit gamification XP metrics, or register duplicate accounts. ServeMATE reserves the right to terminate accounts that violate platform integrity.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: June 17, 2026

1. Donation Finality

As a technology platform facilitating micro-donations directly to registered social causes, all successful donations made through ServeMATE are **final, non-refundable, and non-cancellable** because they are immediately pledged and routed to the corresponding campaign pool.

2. Technical Double-Charges or Failures

In cases of technical issues where your bank account is charged twice or the payment fails midway but the money is debited, the transaction will be auto-refunded by our payment gateway processor (Razorpay) within **5 to 7 business days**.

3. Disputed Payouts

If a designated NGO fails to submit verifiable video proof within the stipulated campaign completion period, the pending campaign pool is frozen. ServeMATE will audit the campaign and, if fraud is found, redistribute the frozen funds to another verified cause in the same category to guarantee donor intent.

4. Support Contact

For any transaction support, failed payment queries, or refund complaints, please email our support desk at info@resence.in.
